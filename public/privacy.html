⚖️ 1. Statutory Framework & Legal Standing

🏛️ This Privacy Policy is published in accordance with the provisions of Rule 3(1) of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, Section 43A of the Information Technology Act, 2000, the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

This Privacy Policy governs the processing, collection, storage, transfer, and protection of digital personal data collected by ExamHub Technologies Private Limited ("ExamHub", "Company", "We", "Us", or "Our"), a company incorporated under the Indian Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office in India, from educational institutions, universities, coaching academies, administrators, faculty, and students ("Data Principals" or "Users").

🤝 2. Data Fiduciary vs. Data Processor

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:

  • The Educational Institution / University ("Client"): Acts as the Data Fiduciary. The Institution determines the purpose and means of processing student data, schedules exams, decides eligibility, and controls candidate records.
  • ExamHub: Acts as the Data Processor. We process digital personal data solely on behalf of, and under valid instructions from, the Data Fiduciary pursuant to a lawful contract.

Note for Students: Your university/college is the primary Data Fiduciary. Requests for correction, deletion, or access to official academic records should be directed to your institution's Controller of Examinations or Registrar.

📦 3. Digital Data We Collect

3.1 Personal Data Provided by Data Fiduciary

The Institution uploads or registers the following candidate details:

  • Full Name, Gender, Date of Birth
  • Institutional Email Address and Mobile Number
  • Roll Number, Enrollment Number, Course / Batch Code
  • Passport Photograph (if enabled by institution for candidate verification)

To ensure fair examination conduct and prevent academic malpractice in accordance with UGC/NTA guidelines, ExamHub logs:

⚙️ 4. Lawful Purpose & Consent

ExamHub processes digital personal data strictly for lawful, specified purposes:

  • Providing secure computer-based testing (CBT) and online examination services
  • Automated evaluation of Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ) and Multiple Select Questions (MSQ)
  • Generating certified transcripts, scorecards, merit lists, and analytics for the Data Fiduciary
  • Preventing examination fraud, impersonation, and unauthorized access
  • Complying with cybersecurity mandates issued by CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team)
🚫 ExamHub does not sell, rent, monetize, or profile candidate personal data for commercial advertising. Data is processed exclusively for educational and examination delivery.

🔐 5. Reasonable Security Practices & Procedures

Pursuant to Section 43A of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, ExamHub maintains comprehensive technical and organizational security controls:

  • Encryption Standards: Data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption; static database volumes are encrypted using AES-256 bits encryption.
  • Password Protection: User authentication credentials are protected using salted bcrypt cryptographic hashes.
  • Session Integrity: Secure JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with mandatory idle timeout enforcement.
  • Infrastructure Security: Enterprise PostgreSQL instance deployed in Indian data center zones with automated backups and firewall protection.
  • Incident Response: Mandatory cybersecurity incident reporting to CERT-In within statutory timelines.

⚖️ 6. Data Principal Rights (Under DPDP Act 2023)

Subject to statutory limits for official examinations, Data Principals enjoy the following statutory rights under Chapter III of the DPDP Act 2023:

  • Right to Access Information (Sec 11): Right to obtain a summary of personal data being processed and identities of processing entities.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure (Sec 12): Right to request correction of inaccurate data or erasure of data no longer necessary for exam administration.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal (Sec 13): Right to readily available means of grievance redressal regarding data obligations.
  • Right to Nominate (Sec 14): Right to nominate an individual to exercise rights in the event of death or incapacity.

Data Principals may submit rights requests to their respective University/Institute Data Fiduciary or directly to our Grievance Officer.

📂 7. Data Retention & Eradication

Personal data is retained only for the duration specified by the Data Fiduciary or required by Indian statutory mandates:

  • Active Exam Data: Retained for the duration of the institutional contract.
  • Cybersecurity Logs: Retained for 180 days as mandated under CERT-In Directions 2022.
  • Eradication Procedure: Post-expiry of retention timelines, data is overwritten using secure cryptographic deletion procedures.

🌐 8. Data Localization & Cross-Border Processing

ExamHub prioritizes storing and processing data within the sovereign territory of the Republic of India.

In accordance with Section 16 of the DPDP Act 2023, personal data may only be transferred to countries or territories outside India that are not restricted by Central Government notification, ensuring equivalent levels of privacy safeguard.
