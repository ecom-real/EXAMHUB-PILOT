Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

1. Cancellations

We believe in a transparent and fair cancellation process. You may cancel your subscription at any time.

  • To cancel your subscription, please log in to your account and navigate to the Billing section, or contact our support team at teamhydroxy@Hotmail.com.
  • Cancellations will take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. You will continue to have access to the service until then.

2. Refunds

Refunds are processed in accordance with the following guidelines:

  • Monthly Subscriptions: We do not offer refunds for monthly subscription payments once the payment has been processed.
  • Annual Subscriptions: If you are on an annual plan and decide to cancel within the first 14 days of your purchase, you are eligible for a full refund. After 14 days, no refunds will be issued for annual subscriptions.
  • One-Time Purchases: For any one-time setup fees or specific custom feature developments, fees are non-refundable once the work has commenced.

3. Processing of Refunds

Approved refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days and will be credited back to the original method of payment used during the purchase.

4. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Refund & Cancellation Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
